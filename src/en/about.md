Gongdao Commons was initiated by the "Gongdao Civilization — Renewal Abroad" team, based in Ukraine, and is planned to operate as a Ukrainian non-profit.

The founding team has its own views, but on this platform it appears as an ordinary participant. Its only roles are to convene the shared charter and to maintain the technical channel. The platform is free for everyone and never charges users in mainland China.
