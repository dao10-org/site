order: 1
---
每一句都有“普通版”和“简短版”。普通版用来理解，简短版用来诵读和随时默念。

## 一、人与自然

<div class="verse">
<p class="short">自然孕育生命，自然爱我。</p>
<p class="en">Nature creates life; Nature loves me.</p>
<p class="full">普通版：人是自然所孕育的生命，自然一直爱着人。</p>
<p class="en">Human life is born of nature, and nature always loves us.</p>
</div>

## 二、人与规律

<div class="verse">
<p class="short">顺道而行，光辉自现。</p>
<p class="en">Follow the Way; light unfolds.</p>
<p class="full">普通版：顺道而行，光辉与美好自然展开。</p>
<p class="en">Walk with the Way, and radiance and beauty unfold naturally.</p>
</div>

## 三、自我与他人

<div class="verse">
<p class="short">爱己爱人，生命光辉。</p>
<p class="en">Love self and others; life shines.</p>
<p class="full">普通版：我珍爱我自己，也祝福他人，生命在其中自然走向光辉与幸福。</p>
<p class="en">I cherish myself and bless others; thus, life naturally moves toward radiance and happiness.</p>
</div>
